# Brightpath Solutions Pvt Ltd - Employee Handbook (SAMPLE DATA)

## 1. Working hours
- Office hours: 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Saturday and Sunday are off.
- Core hours (everyone available): 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
- Late arrival: 3 late marks in a month (after 9:45 AM) count as 1 casual leave.

## 2. Leave policy (per calendar year)
- Casual Leave (CL): 12 days. Maximum 3 days at a stretch. Cannot be carried forward.
- Sick Leave (SL): 8 days. Medical certificate needed if 3 or more consecutive days.
- Earned Leave (EL): 15 days. Up to 30 days can be carried forward. Apply 7 days in advance for 3+ days.
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks paid. Paternity leave: 10 working days paid, to be taken within 3 months of birth.
- Leave is applied through the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager.

## 3. Work from home
- Hybrid policy: up to 2 WFH days per week with manager approval.
- Additional WFH beyond 2 days needs written approval from the department head.
- Employees on probation (first 6 months) can WFH only 1 day per week.

## 4. Holidays
- 12 paid public holidays per year. The list is published every January on the HR portal.
- 2 optional holidays per year can be chosen from the optional list.

## 5. Payroll and reimbursements
- Salary is credited on the last working day of each month.
- Payslips are available on the HR portal by the 2nd of the next month.
- Reimbursement claims (travel, internet, client meals) must be submitted within 30 days with receipts.
- Internet reimbursement: up to Rs. 1,000 per month for hybrid employees.
- Reimbursements are paid with the next month's salary.

## 6. Health insurance
- Group health cover of Rs. 5 lakh for employee, spouse and up to 2 children.
- Cover starts from the date of joining. Add dependents within 30 days of joining or marriage/birth.
- Claims and hospital approvals are handled by the insurance desk, not HR.

## 7. Probation and notice period
- Probation: 6 months. Confirmation is by written letter from HR.
- Notice period: 30 days during probation, 60 days after confirmation.

## 8. Conduct and grievances
- Dress code: business casual. Casual wear on Fridays.
- Workplace harassment concerns go to the Internal Committee at ic@brightpath.example (confidential).
- Other grievances: first to your manager, then to HR at hr@brightpath.example.

## 9. Not covered by this handbook
- Individual salary details, appraisal ratings, legal advice, and visa or immigration matters. Raise an HR ticket.
